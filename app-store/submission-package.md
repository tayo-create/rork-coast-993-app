**Notes (paste as-is, edit the bracketed line):**

Coast 99.3 is the official mobile app of Coast 99.3, a Hip Hop, R&B and Throwbacks radio station in Savannah, Georgia (https://coast993.com). [This app is submitted by the station owner / on behalf of the station with its permission.] The station's website shows the App Store download badge and links the same Instagram account (@coast993sav) used in the app.

No account or login is required.

How to review:
1. Open the app. The Live tab shows what's playing now. Tap the orange play button to start the live stream (our own broadcast, streamed from our AzuraCast host). Audio keeps playing in the background and from the Lock Screen. Share and AirPlay sit beside the play button.
2. Scroll down for Recently Played, which lists songs that just aired.
3. Open the Station tab. Here you can turn on keyword alerts, open our Instagram or website, and find the Privacy Policy and Support links at the bottom.

About keyword alerts: during on-air contests, station staff post a keyword from an internal admin page and opted-in listeners get a push notification. Tapping it opens the keyword with a Copy button. Because alerts are only sent while a contest airs, you may not receive one during review. Allowing notifications from the Station tab is the only setup needed. The "Contests landing soon" row is information only. The app runs no sweepstakes, contests or in-app entries.

The app collects no personal information, has no ads and does no tracking. The only data sent off the device is the anonymous Apple push token, and only when the listener allows notifications.
